SOLMAR — Streets of the Sun is a complete 3D open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser, built with three.js and no build step. Dario "Dash" Voss returns to the seaside city of Solmar to buy back his father's garage from the Harbor Syndicate, one job at a time for fixer Mara Solis. Explore a seeded procedural 7×7 street grid — ~90 buildings, a park with a fountain, a beach and pier with a ferris wheel — on foot or in any of the 24 stealable cars, among traffic and pedestrians who flee crimes. A 1–5 star wanted system brings police car chases and on-foot cops at 3+ stars, plus Busted/Wasted screens, punch and pistol combat, 5 story missions with cash rewards, a live minimap HUD, title/pause screens, localStorage saves, and synthesized WebAudio sound. Controls: WASD move/drive, mouse camera, Shift sprint, Space jump/handbrake, E take or leave a car, Q draw/holster pistol, click punch/shoot, M start the next job, H help, N mute, Esc pause.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
